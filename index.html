CTF & Security Research Team

We’re a CTF and security research team made up of people interested in cybersecurity, problem solving, and understanding how things work. We work together on challenges, share what we learn, and help each other improve along the way.

We spend most of our time around web exploitation, reverse engineering, binary exploitation, cryptography, forensics, OSINT, and other areas of offensive security.

We’re also looking for active members who are genuinely interested in security, CTFs, and learning together. You don’t need to know everything or be an expert in every category. If you’re curious, willing to participate, share what you know, and actually want to be part of the team, we’d be happy to have you around.
